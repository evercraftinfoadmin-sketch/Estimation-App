Privacy Policy

EverCraft Home Improvement (License #1133560) · Last updated October 20, 2026

This policy covers EverCraft CRM, the internal tool our team uses to prepare estimates and invoices and to send them to our clients for review and signature.

Who uses the CRM

Only EverCraft Home Improvement staff sign in to the CRM. Clients do not create accounts; they receive estimates, invoices and signing links by email.

Information we keep

To quote and complete your project we keep your name, phone number, email address, project address, the estimates and invoices we prepare for you, project photos, and documents you sign. When you sign a document online we also record your typed name, your drawn signature, the time, your IP address and browser, and a fingerprint of the signed document, so the signature can be verified later.

Google account access

The CRM connects to the company Gmail account (EverCraft.pro@gmail.com) only to send estimates, invoices and signing notices to our clients. It requests the Gmail "send" permission and the account's email address. It does not read, store, or delete any messages in the mailbox, and it does not access any other Google account. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How we use it

Only to prepare, send and track your estimates, invoices and project documents, and to contact you about your project. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers that host the CRM (Base44, Cloudflare, Google, GitHub) and where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep project records as long as needed for the project, warranty and our legal and tax obligations. You can ask us to correct or delete your information, unless we must keep it by law.
